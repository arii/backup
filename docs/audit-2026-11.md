## Found and fixed in this audit

- **Overstated:** the Light coaching option said "fewer reminders". `feedback.js` and
  the research doc say it does not reduce reminders (chime before voice, no timed
  praise). The label now says so.
- **Hardcoded size:** privacy and offline text said "about 25 MB" without a source.
  The app text now uses `precache.json` totals (and drops the number when unknown).
  A test (`tests/js/offline.test.mjs`) fails if `docs/privacy.html` or `README.md`
  disagree with the real total (26.2 MB, 25.0 MiB, "about 25 MB").
- **Weak e2e check:** "offline: webcam mode starts" passed while the camera had not
  started (source "-"). It now waits for source "webcam" and real head-forward
  metrics. Verified with a fake camera: pose detection works with the network off.
- **Share your score** (#49 item 4, previously not built): `share.js` `scoreCardData`,
  `drawScoreCard`; PNG download and Web Share with files. Needs a minute of tracking.
  It draws only the percentage and best streak, never a camera frame. Tests in
  `tests/js/share.test.mjs`; e2e "share card" checks (screenshot `share-card.png`).
- **Share dialog on devices with Web Share:** the dialog was skipped there, so the
  plain links were unreachable. It now always opens, with a "Share with your device"
  button.
- **Dead code:** `OFFLINE_STATES` removed (unused anywhere). Every other export is
  used in its module or a test.
- **Stale narration:** history comments in specs and the research doc removed.
- **Consent for basic tracking:** decision recorded in `docs/legal-checklist.md`
  (no second dialog; the start-screen notice, the "I understand" step and the browser
  camera prompt come first; a lawyer should confirm).
