    Full safety notice

      1. Allow the camera Your browser asks first.
      2. Download the pose model 15 MB, one time.
      3. Start the model on your computer The page may pause briefly.

      Your browser has WebGL turned off. The webcam will still work, using a simpler pose model on your CPU. It is less accurate and slower, and uses more battery. For the best results, turn WebGL on:
      • GPU access is disabled in chrome://settings (the most common cause): open chrome://settings/system, turn on Use graphics acceleration when available, click Relaunch, then check chrome://gpu.
      • Linux / Wayland: start the browser with --ozone-platform=x11.
      • Firefox: open about:config and set webgl.disabled to false.
      • Strict privacy settings, some extensions, remote desktops and virtual machines can also block WebGL; try another browser or machine.

      Then reload this page. Still stuck? Report a bug.

      Relax your shoulders
      
        Good posture is a relaxed stack, not a squeeze. Pinching your shoulder blades back
        to look upright is tiring for your upper back. Lift your chest a little and let your shoulders hang.
        
        
      
        shoulders -
        
      
        Breath check: breathe in slowly. If your shoulders rise toward your ears, you’re bracing. Stop if anything hurts.
        
      
    
        
    
        
      
        Breaks & prompts
        
      
        Gentle prompts, one at a time. None in the first 5 minutes, at most one every 10 minutes, and never during a pause or a silent test.
        
      
        
      Break and breathing options
      
      
      
        
      
      
        
      
      
        
      
        A webcam cannot see jaw tension. This is a reminder, not a reading. Stop if your jaw hurts.
        
      
      
        
      
      
      
        
      
        
        
      
        
      
        One minute of easy breathing, about 4 seconds in and 6 seconds out, then a breath check. Stop if you feel dizzy.
        
      
        Leaving the camera for 2 minutes or more counts as a break and restarts the timers. These are reminders to rest, not medical advice.
