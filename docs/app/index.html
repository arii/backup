Posture to die for.

Leave this tab open. Slouch, and it will haunt you.

Your webcam, the pose model and the posture logic all run in this tab. No video or landmarks leave your computer. Sit facing the camera with your head and shoulders in view.

Your browser has WebGL turned off, and the pose model needs it.
  • Chrome / Edge: Settings > System > turn on Use graphics acceleration when available, then relaunch the browser. chrome://gpu shows whether WebGL is on.
  • Firefox: open about:config and set webgl.disabled to false.
  • Strict privacy settings, some extensions, remote desktops and virtual machines can also block WebGL; try another browser or machine.

Then reload this page. Try the demo works without WebGL. Still stuck? Report a bug.
