Privacy and safety

Short version: your camera feed is analyzed in this tab and never uploaded or saved. No cookies, no analytics, no accounts. This page is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

What runs on your computer

The webcam, the pose model and the posture logic all run in your browser tab. Video frames and body landmarks are held in memory for a moment, then dropped. They are not uploaded and not written to disk. The desktop version of BackUp runs entirely on your machine and keeps working with the network unplugged.

Which servers the page contacts

Like any website, each of these sees your IP address, browser user agent and the page that referred you:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses under its own privacy statement.

The MediaPipe runtime (@mediapipe/tasks-vision, code and WebAssembly) and the pose model file are served from this site, so no other server is contacted when you start the camera or demo. The same holds for the fallback used when WebGL is off (TensorFlow.js with its WebAssembly backend, and the MoveNet Lightning model): it is downloaded from this site only when needed, and runs in your tab. Their versions and checksums are listed in the vendor README.

The Share and Sponsor buttons are plain links. Your browser contacts Bluesky, Mastodon, LinkedIn, X, your email app or GitHub Sponsors only if you open one of them. The page loads no share widgets, counters or other third-party scripts.

The app does not send your settings, scores, calibration or any measurements to any server.

What is stored in your browser, and how to clear it

The app keeps data in your browser's local storage under the key posture-guard:v1. It has no expiry. It holds:

  • your calibration (good and slouch reference values, not images);
  • daily good and slouch totals, plus counters per day: seconds with relaxed shoulders (only with the shoulder and breathing consent), the longest good run, bracing reminders and breaks taken. Days are dated in your local time (older days used UTC);
  • silent test results and the count of shoulder-creep reminders;
  • your settings and voice choice, and the identifier your browser gives the camera that last worked, so the next start can pick the same one (it is forgotten if that camera is gone);
  • the times you last snoozed, dismissed or muted a reminder (up to 20, used only to offer light coaching), and when you declined that offer;
  • your setup progress (which setup steps you finished or skipped), whether the first breath check offer was shown and how often, and a daily water count once the water reminder exists. The water count comes from your own taps on I drank; the camera does not detect drinking;
  • usage counts for the sponsor prompt (number of sessions, the first day you used the app, and how often the prompt was shown or dismissed), triggered by these local counts only and never sent anywhere;
  • if you opt in to the shoulder and breathing features, a dated consent record (version and date);
  • if you switch on Share my weekly score, a dated consent record and the nickname you chose;
  • any custom sound files you upload, stored as base64 data.

Earlier versions offered Make available offline; it is no longer offered. If you used it, the browser keeps a copy of the app's own files (code, the pose models, voice clips, about 32 MB) in its cache storage, managed by a service worker. It holds no video, images or measurements. No service worker is installed otherwise. Like any website, your browser may keep the pose model in its normal HTTP cache after you start the app, and also after you point at, focus or press the "Open the app" link, even if you never start it. This is not the Offline mode and holds no video.

To remove it all, open the app, then use Delete my data in Settings, under My week (this also removes any offline copy). You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings. Nothing is stored in cookies.

Share links for your weekly score

Creating these links is no longer offered; opening a link someone sent you still works. It was off until you ticked Share my weekly score and agree in a dialog that shows the exact record. A link then holds six fields: a format number, the week, the nickname you chose, tracked minutes, a consistency score and breaks taken. It holds no video, images, landmarks, per-minute data, name, email or device identifier.

There is no server for this. The record sits in the part of the link after the #, which browsers do not send to the website, and you copy and send the link yourself. This site never receives it. Anyone you give the link to keeps a copy that you cannot take back, and a link can be edited by whoever holds it, so the numbers prove nothing. Opening a friend's link only shows it on your screen. It is not saved. Turning the option off removes the consent record and nickname. Delete my data removes them too.

You can Export your progress as JSON from My week. It happens in your browser.

Biometric retention and destruction

We do not retain any face geometry. Each camera frame is discarded after processing, in memory, in your browser. Only simple derived scores are stored in your browser, such as daily good and slouch seconds and a count of shoulder-creep reminders. We do not store landmark points, images, video or embeddings. We do no identity matching: there is no face recognition, no accounts and no profile keyed to a face. The pose model returns body and head landmark points (including nose, eyes and ears) only to compute posture angles in the tab. Anything stored is deleted when you use Delete my data or clear this site's data in your browser, and consent can be withdrawn in the app under Breaks & prompts.

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

BackUp has features that look at shoulder and neck position and time a breathing exercise (breath check, Breathe with me, breathing moments and shoulder-creep reminders). Information like this can count as consumer health data under laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act. This is a plain-language policy for those features. It is not legal advice.

The shoulder features (breath check verdict, shoulder-creep reminders and relaxed-shoulders time) are unavailable when BackUp runs on the simpler fallback model, which is used when hardware acceleration (WebGL) is off. Breathe with me still works there because it is only a timer.

  • What we handle: shoulder, neck and nose positions from your camera picture, and the simple results (for example, a count of shoulder-creep reminders and your daily posture totals).
  • Where: only in your browser tab, on your device. We do not collect it on a server, and we do not sell, share or send it to anyone. There are no accounts, analytics or advertising.
  • Consent: these features stay off until you agree on a consent screen. We keep a dated record of that agreement in your browser. You can withdraw it at any time in the app (Breaks & prompts), which turns the features off again.
  • Deletion: Delete my data in the app removes everything stored, including the consent record and the offline copy.
  • Changes: if this ever changes (for example, sending any health data to a server), we will ask for your consent again first.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

None. BackUp sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

What a bug report shares

Report a bug opens a pre-filled issue on GitHub, in a public repository. Nothing is sent until you press Submit on github.com, and you can edit or delete any line first. The diagnostics contain your browser user agent, language, screen size, whether WebGL works and its version (not your GPU model), the app's state and settings, whether calibration exists, and the last few error messages with URLs and file paths removed. They never contain video, images, landmarks or your uploaded sounds.

About and safety

BackUp is a general wellness reminder, not a medical device, and it doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. It also doesn't promise results: a nudge to sit up is a nudge, not a cure. Don't hold a rigid posture, move around often, and if something hurts, stop and see a health professional. If you have an existing back, neck or shoulder condition, are pregnant, or are recovering from an injury, check with a professional first. The relax panel and breath check are gentle prompts. Stop if anything hurts.
